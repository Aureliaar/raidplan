Raidplan · Proposal

Selection, drag & contextual handles rework

Replaces the single-corner Transformer with type-aware grips, makes anchored drags legible, adds radial snapping, and gives multi-select a real gizmo.
1
Separate select from drag
Mousedown selects; the drag only latches after ~4 screen px of movement (zoom-independent). Kills the accidental 1–2 unit nudge from a plain click.
2
Contextual handles per entity type
Drop the one-size Konva Transformer for a custom handle layer. Rotation moves onto the selection ring itself; donuts grow an inner-radius grip, cones an angle grip, tethers a range grip. A live readout with Shift for fine steps covers backlog 4.2 / 4.3 / 4.4. → see Contextual Handles artboard.
3
Anchored-bait drag feedback
Dragging a bait with an anchor draws a dashed leash from the anchor's resolved point, with the offset readout on it. Dropping near the anchor snaps the offset to zero — “just follow the target again”. → Drag Feedback artboard.
4
Radial snapping during drag
Snap angle-from-center to 45° spokes, radius to nearby entities' radii, and point-snap to waymarks. Guides appear only while snapped; Alt disables. FFXIV positioning is radial — this is the snap that matters.
5
Multi-select transform gizmo
2+ selected shows a group ring at the centroid: inside = translate, ring = rotate around arena center, corner grip = scale radii. Makes the symmetry mode legible before the drag, not via the after-the-fact caption. → Group Gizmo artboard.
