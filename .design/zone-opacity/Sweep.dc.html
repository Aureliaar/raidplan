How it reads
Direction is visible at a glance: which way a beam travels, which side a cleave comes from. Stepping through a plan shows how close each cast is to landing.
Tradeoff
Needs a “from” for every shape; beams and cones have one, plain rectangles do not. A cast that appears and goes off in the same step has no sweep to show.
