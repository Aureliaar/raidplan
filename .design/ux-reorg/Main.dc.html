Raidplan · Proposal

Editor chrome reorg — the pareto wall

In the screenshot's viewport (1536×736 css px at 125% zoom) the arena gets a 504 px square. Every high-value item below is about giving that square back to the arena and putting controls where they act. Scores are 1–5, from the screenshot plus a read of Editor.tsx.
Value vs effort
Wave 1 · give the arena its window back · ~1 day
A
One-row header
The header is one flex-wrap row that wraps into two because the title and encounter inputs are 280 + 180 px wide. Make both click-to-edit text (a field only while focused), park rev · live · owner under the title in 10 px, and pin the symmetry / transform / drag-scope cluster in the centre. Undo, redo, History and Share sit right. The header goes from 84 to 48 px. → Proposed artboard.
B
Fold the Preview strip into the rail
The strip spends a full row on “No Variant split active in this Step”. The edit destination already shows as the mech banner over the canvas; the variant preview pills move to the top of the step rail, above the mechanic list, and appear only when a split is active. Saves 38 px more, 74 px in all. Together with A and C the arena square grows from 504 to ~664 px at the screenshot's window, 1.74× the area.
C
Group drop zones on demand
The 200 px column of seven dashed cards exists for two jobs: drop a palette item on a group, and select a group. Selection becomes a row of group chips at the top of the Add panel (still drop targets, still ring their members on hover). The big targets appear only while something is in hand, as an overlay strip on the canvas edge, then vanish. Reclaims 216 px of width. → Proposed and Add panel artboards.
Wave 2 · density where it counts · ~2 days
E
Slim timeline lanes
The rail is 236 px of steps plus 82 px per Beat lane with its gap, so five overlapping Beats push it to 646 px and still truncate labels to “Add…”. Lanes go to 56 px, the label wraps to two lines at 10 px, the count sits in the corner, and the BOOM word becomes a 3 px amber baseline with the word in the tooltip. Same drag semantics; the rail stops scaling lanes down to fit, and labels stay readable. → Timeline lanes artboard.
D
Dense Add palette
Sixteen two-column tiles with 30 px glyphs plus a five-line help paragraph means the palette scrolls on a 1030 px window. Three columns, 22 px glyphs, grouped Actors / Zones / Baits / Tethers & notes, help behind a ? hover, and Layout + Encounter markers merged into one compact Arena section above the existing arena fields. Everything fits without scrolling. → Add panel artboard.
I
Beat editor moves to the right aside
The open-Beat card (Location, Variants, colours, deal debuffs, done editing) renders inside the left rail and pushes the timeline down. The right aside already swaps Add ↔ Inspector on selection; a selected Beat becomes a third state there, so the rail stays a timeline and the inspector is the one place for “what is selected”. Backlog 2.1 / 2.2 already want the inspector for this.
Wave 3 · cheap polish · half a day
GStep actions on hover. The ◇ D + × icons show on any hovered row, not just the current one, with named tooltips; a ghost “+ step” row closes each mechanic.
HShortcut sheet. Seventeen bindings exist and none are listed anywhere; ? opens a one-screen overlay. → Shortcuts artboard.
FNotes drawer. The 128 px Step-notes textarea duplicates the draggable notes card on the canvas; collapse it to an auto-growing two-line field.
Deliberately not on the wall
K draggable variant ranges (backlog 2.4) and L notes / arrows as parts (5.5) are real wants but each is a model change, not a chrome change. J a floating tool strip over the canvas only pays off once A frees the header; revisit after wave 1.
