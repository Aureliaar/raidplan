Ideas 3 & 4
Anchor leash & radial snapping
Anchor leash: dragging an anchored bait draws a dashed leash from the anchor's resolved point with the live offset on it. The faint ring around the target is the snap-back zone — drop inside it and the offset returns to zero.
Radial snap: the drag snaps angle-from-center to 45° spokes and radius to nearby entities (D2 lands on D1's ring, on the NE spoke). Guides render only while snapped, in the quiet pin gray/sky already in use.
